## Step 1 — Requirements

- A/B partitions with boot flag
- Verify signature + version before swap
- Bootloader health GPIO/UART marker
- Driver suspend: save regs, gate clk, pin tri-state

## Step 4 — Failure modes

- Power loss mid-OTA — keep old slot valid until atomic swap
- Resume without restore — peripheral mismatch
- Downgrade attack — monotonic version

## Step 5 — Tradeoffs table

| OTA | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Dual bank | Safe rollback | 2× flash |
| Patch | Less space | Complex delta |

## Step 6 — Numbers / budgets

- 128 KB BL + 1 MB app typical; OTA chunk 4 KB aligned to flash page

## Step 7 — Follow-ups

**Q: Anti-brick?**  
**A:** Never erase running slot until verify complete; keep boot pin recovery.

**Q: Secure load factory vs field?**  
**A:** Factory: JTAG lock after fuse; field: signed OTA only.

## Step 8 — TI story hook

Drive OTA: verified image in inactive bank, atomic flag swap — bricked units recovered via UART BL after bad OTA taught us verify-before-erase.
